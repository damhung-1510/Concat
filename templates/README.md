# Thơ Hoài Cổ templates

Template bundles for the app's template library (copy a folder into the app's `templates/`
folder, or regenerate them with `scripts/make-lofi-templates.py`).

| Folder | Use |
|---|---|
| `tho-hoai-co` | A short verse, still, on one page, over a dimmed video |
| `tho-hoai-co-chay-chu-2-trang` | A verse of up to eight lines, two pages whose words arrive as they are read |
| `tho-hoai-co-chay-chu-nhieu-trang` | A longer verse, three pages of up to six lines |
| `tho-tram-1990s` | A text-only verse card on paper, no video |

`scripts/apply-tho-template.py` stretches one of these to a video's length and renders it.
